Peak values and AUC₀–end are numerical estimates. AUC₀–∞ is theoretical. Interval Tmax is relative to its dose; the next dose is excluded.

Model assumptions & interpretation ＋

Linear, time-invariant pharmacokinetics with first-order elimination from the central compartment. Oral doses have first-order absorption, constant bioavailability, and no lag time. Repeated doses use superposition. Concentrations represent the central compartment.

Disposition half-life describes elimination/distribution. Apparent oral half-life uses the slower of absorption and terminal disposition; a longer apparent half-life indicates absorption-limited (flip-flop) kinetics. For zero dose or F = 0 these are model rate descriptors, not an observable concentration slope. Log scale omits true zero values.
